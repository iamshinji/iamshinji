### Selected work

My working repositories are private. Here is what they do, without the code; details on [the portfolio page](https://gael.palie.fr/en/#portfolio).

- **Standardised crisis communication** (banking): message templates, update cadence and a recipient matrix during major incidents, for more transparency and a shorter time to resolution.
- **Automated ITSM reporting** (two banking clients, ServiceNow): dashboards and scheduled reports for the Incident, Request, Change and Problem processes, replacing manual reporting.
- **MCP servers and AI-assisted processes** (homelab): MCP servers that connect an AI assistant to real tools and data, and a comparison between self-hosted models and those of the major providers.

- **Exam practice platform**: mock exams, quizzes and exercises for candidates in the ITIL and AI courses I teach, with personal access managed by Authentik.
